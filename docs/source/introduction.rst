##############
 Introduction
##############

**Kora** is a web application designed to support the standardized management, analysis, and utilization of plant genetic resources (PGRs), with a primary focus on cultivated crops. Built with simplicity in mind and aligned with international data standards, Kora streamlines the work of a diverse and multidisciplinary range of stakeholders involved in PGR conservation, evaluation, and genetic improvement. It enables institutions, researchers, and practitioners to maintain high-quality datasets, execute data-driven workflows, and facilitate informed decisions related to germplasm utilization.

.. caution::

   This project is under active development.
